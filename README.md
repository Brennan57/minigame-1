# Minigame 1
## Devlog

The relationship between Components, GameObjects, and Scenes are simmilar to that of the relationship between ingredients, food, and a meal. Componants make up a GameObject, just like how ingredients make up foods. Lettuce, crutons, and dressing make up a salad; spices, rice, and meat make up curry; blended fruits, juice, and many other ingredients make up a smoothie. GameObjects make up Scenes, just like how diffrent foods make up a meal. A balenced meal includes many diffrent food groups. Salad gives the vegies. Curry gives the grains and protein. Smoothies give the fruits and sometimes dairy. The three mixed together make up a meal. Componants are ingredients that make up the food. Gameobjects are the foods that make up the meal. The Scenes are the meals, fully completed because of the things that make it up.
